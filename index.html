これまで外国人に向けた茶道・華道・日本語の指導に携わってきた。日本文化国際親善事業として、ニューヨーク、バンクーバー、ロンドン、スペイン領カナリア諸島に毎年と講師、指導に当たっている。また、日本人に向けた伝統文化としての茶道の指導を目的として、毎年おもてなし講座を開催している。

The professor of Japanese tea, the Kōdō, flower arrangement, and Japanese dinner. Also, international translator.

Tea ceremony — Chadō — is a traditional part of Japanese culture. Chadō can be explained as a synthesis of many art forms. It includes the spirit of Zen, Kōdō, flower arrangement, architecture, calligraphy, industrial crafts, tea ceremony dinner, and Japanese confections. In the tea ceremony, the host serves a bowl of tea that includes the spirits of all these art forms.

Learning about the tea ceremony is not only the way of serving or drinking tea, but it is also considered mental training for the spirit of Zen. Let's try to learn the mindset of hospitality through the art of tea ceremony.
